Our Story

Three careers.
Two pivots. One mission.

The Adapt and Win System was built by someone who has lived exactly what it was designed to address.

32 Years

Healthcare Sales & Marketing

Progressively senior roles at two major healthcare companies. MBA earned while working full-time. Built deep expertise in positioning, messaging, and understanding what moves people to act.

The Turn

Downsizing Round

After 32 years, a restructuring ended it. Not performance. Not failure. Just the game changing without warning — the same experience millions of workers are having right now with AI.

Career Two

Education

Pivoted to teaching. Used his background to build a second career from scratch. Retired from that too — on his own terms.

Now

The Adapt and Win System

When AI began reshaping every industry he had worked in — faster than anyone predicted, with less warning than anyone deserved — he moved into the digital marketplace and built the tools he wished had existed when he needed them.

The Adapt and Win System was not built by someone who has only read about workplace disruption. It was built by someone who has been through it — who knows what it feels like the morning after a downsizing round, and who figured out how to come back stronger. Twice.

The tools for fighting back exist. Most workers just don't know how to use them yet. That's the gap this system was built to close — not with theory, but with a guided experience that gives every worker the clarity, connections, and AI skills to compete in the workplace that exists right now.

The AI-driven workplace is not coming. It's here. The workers who adapt first will gain an advantage that compounds over time. The ones who wait will find the gap harder to close with every passing month.

This is built for the workers who intend to be on the right side of it.

"You didn't lose. The game changed. Now let's go win it."
— Mark Guren, Founder, Adapt and Win System
