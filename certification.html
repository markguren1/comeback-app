Your Submission

Three parts. All required.

Missing any part results in an automatic incomplete. All three must be submitted together.

1

The Work Product

A completed, real-world deliverable relevant to your professional situation. Something you could show a hiring manager, client, or employer today.

Primary deliverable
2

The Prompt Log

Every instruction you gave the AI, in order — including prompts you rejected or revised. This is where your judgment and direction show up. It cannot be faked convincingly.

Most important element
3

The Direction Statement

200–350 words in your own voice covering one decision that improved your output, one moment you rejected AI output and why, and how you'd describe your AI working style to a hiring manager.

Your voice
How You Are Evaluated
Three standards. Each must be met independently. Failing any one results in a Fail overall. Result is Pass or Fail — no partial credit.
Output Quality
Your work product is at a level a real employer, hiring manager, or professional contact would find genuinely useful. It is specific to your situation — not generic advice any AI would produce from a one-line prompt.
Direction Quality
Your prompt log shows you were operating the AI — not following it. There is evidence of iteration, rejection of weak output, context-setting, and refinement. One prompt followed by unedited output does not pass.
Coherence
Your work product, prompt log, and direction statement tell a consistent story. The sophistication of your log matches the quality of your output. The three parts feel like they came from a single lived process — not assembled separately.

On AI Use and Integrity

This certification does not prohibit using AI to produce any part of your work product. Using AI extensively — even to complete the entire deliverable — is acceptable if done with skill and direction. That is the point.

What is evaluated is whether a skilled human mind was in charge of the process. Your prompt log is the record of that. It is very difficult to convincingly fake a sophisticated prompting process without actually doing the work.

Important: If your submission is rejected because the evaluation panel determines there was no meaningful human direction — a shallow prompt log, no evidence of iteration or judgment, or a clear mismatch between your prompts and your output — your payment is non-refundable. If your project plan is not approved at the application stage, you pay nothing. If you submit a good-faith project that does not pass, you may resubmit once at no additional charge.
