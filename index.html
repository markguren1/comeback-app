Personality Quiz

What's your communication style?

8 quick questions. No right or wrong answers. Find out if you're a Driver, Analytical, Expressive, or Amiable — and what that means for how you work.

🔴

Driver

Decisive. Direct. Results-focused.

🔵

Analytical

🟣

Expressive

Energetic. Inspiring. Big-picture.

🟢

Amiable

Supportive. Steady. People-first.

Question 1 of 8

🔥
Hot Buttons — What motivates each style Driver · Analytical · Expressive · Amiable
▼
🔴 Driver
  • ⚡
    Lead with outcomes — Skip the backstory. Start with the result, deadline, and decision needed.
  • 🎯
    Respect their time — Be concise. Give two options. They decide fast — make it easy.
  • 🏆
    Show confidence — Own your position. Don't hedge. Make your case and stand behind it.
🔵 Analytical
  • 📊
    Provide facts and data — Back up claims with numbers or logic. Vague assertions create skepticism.
  • 🔍
    Explain the why — They want the reasoning, not just the conclusion. Show your process.
  • ⏳
    Give them time — Don't pressure for immediate answers. Follow up in writing.
🟣 Expressive
  • 🎉
    Bring energy and stories — Open with a vision or story before the details. Energy is contagious to them.
  • 💡
    Make it social and fun — Dry transactional communication shuts them down. Make them feel part of something.
  • 🚀
    Paint the vision — Show the big picture first. They're motivated by possibility — "what could be" before "how."
🟢 Amiable
  • 🤝
    Build trust first — Connect personally before getting to business. Relationships come before transactions.
  • 👂
    Invite their input — They won't speak up unless asked. "What do you think?" goes a long way.
  • 🕊️
    Keep the tone kind — Bluntness shuts them down. They'll disengage rather than push back.
🔗
Compatibility — Who clicks, who clashes Best matches · Challenging pairs · Why it matters at work
▼

Knowing who you click with — and who creates friction — gives you an edge in interviews, team dynamics, and working with managers.

  • 🔴
    Driver Best with Amiable. Hard with Expressive — both want the spotlight, neither slows down.
  • 🔵
    Analytical Best with Driver. Hard with Amiable — neither pushes decisions forward.
  • 🟣
    Expressive Best with Amiable. Hard with Analytical — cold vs. unfocused friction.
  • 🟢
    Amiable Best with Expressive. Hard with Driver — bluntness vs. slow pace.
Interview Application

Reading your interviewer's style

  • 🔴
    Driver Gets to results fast. Match their pace. Use numbers. Don't ramble.
  • 🔵
    Analytical Asks process questions. Show methodology. Walk through your thinking step by step.
  • 🟣
    Expressive Warm and conversational. Match their energy. Tell stories. Show enthusiasm.
  • 🟢
    Amiable Focuses on team fit. Lead with collaboration. Don't come across as aggressive.

Prompt Formula

Role + Context + Goal + Standard + Format

The clearer your direction, the more useful the first draft becomes. This formula works for any AI tool — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini.

  • RoleTell AI what expert to be. "Act as a senior healthcare contract manager..."
  • ContextWhat situation are you in? What constraints matter?
  • GoalWhat specific outcome do you need?
  • StandardWhat does excellent output look like? Define the quality bar first.
  • FormatBullets, table, email, one-pager? Tell AI how to deliver it.
Guided Practice

Build your own progression

Step 1: Write a basic prompt you commonly use.

Step 2: Rewrite it with defined outcome, constraints, and audience.

Step 3: Convert it into a repeatable system or template.

The goal is not longer prompts. The goal is structured thinking that produces repeatable results.
